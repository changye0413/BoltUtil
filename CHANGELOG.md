## Product capability notes

- BoltUtil supports order creation, hosted checkout, on-chain payment matching, confirmation tracking, signed Webhooks, delivery retry, and reconciliation workflows.
- Supported network options are TRC20, ERC20, BEP20, POLYGON, SOLANA and BASE, with network-specific USDT/USDC representations. Current requirements and availability are maintained in the official documentation.

# Contributor covenant code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this public documentation and integration community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus feedback on integration behavior and documented evidence.
- Protect merchant, customer, account, wallet, and credential information.
- Accept correction and help keep technical information accurate.

Harassment, threats, deliberate disclosure of sensitive data, spam, impersonation, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@boltutil.com**. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repository Issues, Discussions, pull requests, and other public project spaces.
